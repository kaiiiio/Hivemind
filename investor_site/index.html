Architecture Scope

This is not a stock picker. It is a capital-markets operating system.

HIVEMIND covers Indian capital markets across shares and equities, debt, commodities, FX, rates, derivatives for hedging, macro policy, procurement flows, market-structure events, and price action. The system should answer a harder question than "which security has the highest score?"

What changed across capital markets, who benefits or loses, how material is it, what can hedge or amplify the risk, and what evidence supports or weakens the view?

01 Product Thesis

Markets do not move as one clean signal. They move as situations.

A normal screener sees a price jump. A news scraper sees a headline. A generic LLM sees a story. HIVEMIND must see a situation forming across evidence, time, relationships, market behavior, valuation, and portfolio exposure.

event + materiality + exposure + market behavior + valuation + evidence quality

The operating model is evidence-first: raw evidence exists before parsing, summarization, embeddings, graph edges, agent reasoning, or investor-facing claims. AI improves investigation; it does not become the source of truth.

02 Terminal Product

The end product is a professional capital-markets terminal.

The terminal is not a passive dashboard and not a chatbot. It is a workspace where a user can search, investigate, monitor, replay, and manage portfolios around market situations.

04 Swarm-First Architecture

AI swarms are not a late layer. They operate across the pipeline.

HIVEMIND starts with 10-15 core agents and workers, then scales to 30-40 specialists through routing. The important part is not running every agent on everything. The important part is selecting the smallest useful specialist set for the situation, research job, or portfolio question.

Data Ingestion

Monitor sources, parse filings, resolve entities, detect schema drift, audit missing records.

Search And Research

Generate targeted queries, classify source quality, triangulate evidence, verify against official sources.

Graph Construction

Extract relationships, merge aliases, link company-sector-policy-tender-customer-commodity edges.

Situation Detection

Explain why an event belongs to a situation family and identify missing facts.

Quant Validation

Run event studies, abnormal-return checks, regime filters, peer comparisons, and liquidity checks.

Risk And Valuation

Challenge the thesis, detect crowded/euphoric setups, flag balance sheet and governance risk.

Final Synthesis

Preserve opportunity/risk uncertainty and publish an evidence-cited situation brief.

Markets are multi-causal. One model cannot reliably parse filings, map sector exposure, test price action, inspect valuation, challenge risks, and explain hedge context in one pass. Specialist agents give each role its own tools, schemas, prompts, budgets, and quality gates.

Example: crude + INR + rates shock

  1. Market workers detect crude, USD/INR, G-sec yields, and sector breadth moving together.
  2. The router activates macro, commodity, credit, equity exposure, derivatives/hedge, valuation, market behavior, and risk agents.
  3. The memory broker sends only relevant exposures, filings, peer history, hedge history, similar event windows, and source IDs.
  4. Specialists disagree productively: beneficiaries, margin losers, funding risk, hedge availability, price/liquidity confirmation, valuation stretch.
  5. The synthesis agent publishes a terminal situation page with evidence, confidence, uncertainty, hedge context, and monitoring triggers.
  6. The replay loop later scores early, late, false positive, useful watch, missed catalyst, and which agents were useful or noisy.

06 Deep Research Jobs

A complex prompt becomes a governed workflow.

A user prompt such as "scan broker reports and Q4 earnings for small/mid-cap stocks with high upside and strict financial guardrails" must not run as one long prompt. It compiles into a research job object with universe, dates, source rules, hard filters, formulas, agent plan, stop rules, output schema, and memory writeback.

If a source cannot be verified, the system marks EVIDENCE_GAP. AI can find, parse, compare, debate, and summarize evidence. It is not the source of truth.

07 Knowledge And Memory

Do not dump everything into one vector store and call it memory.

User prompts, analyst notes, research reports, observations, and post-mortems become knowledge artifacts. They are not automatically facts. They become templates, checklists, candidate source lists, situation patterns, post-mortem lessons, and evaluation datasets.

Raw Evidence LakeExact filings, PDFs, API payloads, pages, RSS items, tables, transcripts, checksums.Truth and replay.

Stored knowledge does not automatically change GPT, Gemini, Qwen, or DeepSeek model weights. It makes HIVEMIND smarter because retrieval, routing, critique, scoring, thresholds, and memory improve around the model.

09 Evidence Layers

Every important output must be replayable from raw evidence.

Bronze

Exact raw evidence: HTML, PDFs, CSV, RSS items, API payloads, search result snapshots.

Silver

Parsed and normalized evidence: extracted text, tables, entity links, cleaned filings, normalized events.

Gold

Decision-grade objects: canonical events, features, situations, briefs, alerts, outcome labels.

  • Never skip bronze storage.
  • Never let AI overwrite raw evidence.
  • Every gold object traces back to evidence IDs.
  • All AI outputs store provider, model, prompt version, evidence IDs, and schema version.
  • Old raw evidence must be replayable when parsers or models improve.

11 Agent Operating Model

Agents are executable roles with mandates, tools, biases, and quality gates.

Agent personality is not branding. It means each agent has a mandate, productive bias, retrieval order, evidence-weighting policy, known blind spots, allowed tools, output schema, and quality gate.

12 Token-Efficient Intelligence

The AI budget is controlled by design, not hope.

Deterministic First Pass

Rules, parsers, source metadata, joins, and quant features run before LLM calls.

Model Routing

Cheap models classify and extract; stronger models handle high-value reasoning.

Prompt Caching

Company, sector, source, and instruction packs should be cacheable.

Evidence IDs

Agents receive compact references and relevant snippets, not full raw documents by default.

Graph Retrieval

Bring only relevant neighborhoods, not the whole corpus.

Hybrid Retrieval

BM25 + vector + graph avoids stuffing irrelevant context into prompts.

Context Compression

Retrieved evidence becomes source-cited context packs.

Confidence Gates

Escalate only when confidence is low, evidence conflicts, or situation value is high.

13 Backtesting And Quant Validation

AI explains situations. Quant validation checks whether they mattered.

Backtesting asks whether similar events created abnormal returns, whether the move was issuer-specific or regime-driven, whether volume and delivery confirmed, whether the event decayed or persisted, whether hedges helped, which features predicted false positives, and whether valuation was already euphoric.

T-5 to T-1
pre-event accumulation T0
event day T+1 to T+5
immediate reaction T+6 to T+20
swing follow-through
  • Point-in-time data only.
  • Corporate actions and instrument identity handled before returns are computed.
  • No survivorship bias.
  • Explicit costs, slippage, liquidity, and hedge assumptions.
  • Walk-forward testing for thresholds and ranking.
  • Report hit rate, payoff distribution, drawdown, turnover, exposure, and deflated Sharpe where relevant.

15 First Build Sequence

Build from evidence spine to portfolio-aware swarm terminal.

  1. Instrument universe: listed issuers/securities, ISIN identity, BSE/NSE mappings, market-cap buckets, debt/rates/commodity/FX/derivatives references.
  2. Raw evidence store: source, URL, timestamps, checksum, raw text/path.
  3. Official events: BSE/NSE announcements, RSS/circulars, company filings.
  4. EOD market behavior: bhavcopy, OHLCV, delivery, turnover, corporate actions.
  5. Situation V1: order wins, results, promoter actions, capex, ratings, unexplained price action.
  6. Macro/policy sources: SEBI, RBI, MoSPI/eSankhyiki, PIB, ministries.
  7. Market-structure lane: MSCI/FTSE/Nifty/BSE index events, F&O, ASM/GSM, block/bulk.
  8. Cross-asset lane: crude, gas, metals, USD/INR, yields, global risk, geopolitical shocks.
  9. Tender lane: CPPP, GeM, IREPS, PSU and sector portals.
  10. Ingestion swarm: parser, entity resolver, source health, search audit, graph writer.
  11. AI layer: provider interface, model router, memory/retrieval, specialist agents, synthesis agent.
  12. Quant validation: event-study replay, post-mortems, false-positive labels.

16 Moat

The moat is not "we use AI."

The moat compounds through proprietary cleaned Indian market evidence and the operating memory around it.
